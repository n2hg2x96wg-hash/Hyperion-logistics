# Carrier photo sources (internal record)

Photos on the carrier cards (homepage `#carriers` and `carriers.html`), mapped by carrier id in `CARRIER_PHOTOS`
(`ui/carrier-media.js`). This file is a record for the site owner; it is not linked from the site.

Every photo is either in the **public domain** or dedicated to it under **CC0 1.0** by its author, as stated on its
Wikimedia Commons file page. Neither requires attribution, so the cards show no credit line and the site has no
credits page. Each was checked on its file page (licence, subject) and by eye before use, and resized to 480 and 960 px
wide in AVIF, WebP and JPEG (metadata stripped). Carrier names and logos visible in the photos remain trademarks of
their owners, as the footer says.

## Carriers without a photo

2GoExpress, Aramex, GIG Logistics, Red Star Express: no public-domain or CC0 photo of the carrier itself was found, so these cards use the name-led card
(carrier name on the brand colour). A photo of another carrier or an unbranded stand-in is never used.

## Adding or replacing a photo

Use only a photo you own, one licensed to Hyperion Logistics for this use, or one in the public domain / under CC0. Do
not use CC BY or CC BY-SA photos: those licences require a visible credit. Add `<name>-480/960` in AVIF, WebP and JPEG
here, map it by carrier id in `CARRIER_PHOTOS` with its `license` and `source`, and add a row to the table above.
