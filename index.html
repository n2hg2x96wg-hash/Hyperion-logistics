    About Hyperion

    Logistics you can follow, step by step

    Hyperion Logistics moves shipments by express, air, ocean and road through established carriers. Our operations team records every milestone, so the status you see is the status we have.

    When something changes, your tracking page shows it without a refresh. When a delay or exception happens, we say so plainly.

    Talk to our team
    • Recorded positions, clearly labelledRecorded positions are never shown as live. Only verified carrier or GPS data is marked live. Nothing is estimated or simulated.
    • Private by defaultShipment details are checked on our servers; only what you are allowed to see reaches your browser.
    • Honest delivery estimatesAn estimated date is shown only when one is known; otherwise we tell you it is not available yet.
