How these numbers are counted

Why Hyperion

Clear, honest tracking

Recorded positions, clearly labelled

You see the last recorded location, when it was recorded and where it came from. A position is called live only when the update is recent.

Updates without refreshing

Your tracking page updates automatically when operations records a new status, location or event.

Private by default

Shipment details are checked on our servers and only what you are allowed to see is sent to your browser. Nothing is shared publicly without approval.

Delivery estimates you can trust

An estimated date or window is shown only when one is known. Otherwise we say the estimate is unavailable.

Express, freight and custom transport

Parcel carriers, ocean freight lines, regional couriers and Hyperion-arranged custom transport.

Real people when you need them

Questions about a shipment? Contact Customer Support from your tracking page or by email.

How it works

Three steps

STEP 1

Enter your tracking number

Use the number from your booking confirmation, for example HY-123456.

STEP 2

See where it stands

Status, last recorded location, route map, timeline and delivery estimate in one view.

STEP 3

Keep the page open

New updates appear on their own. Close the page and nothing about your shipment is kept on your device.
